# Privilege Escalation

## Overview

Privilege Escalation is the phase where an attacker attempts to obtain elevated permissions after successfully gaining initial access to a target system.

In this simulated incident involving the NAD Cybersecurity Institute, the attacker leveraged a successful SSH login to enumerate privilege escalation vectors and ultimately obtain root-level access. The investigation focused on identifying privilege escalation techniques, validating root access, and correlating endpoint detections generated by Wazuh.

---

# Objectives

- Enumerate privilege escalation opportunities.
- Identify SUID binaries that may be abused.
- Obtain root-level privileges.
- Investigate sudo activity.
- Validate privilege escalation using Wazuh.

---

# Business Scenario

Following successful authentication to the Linux server, the attacker began searching for methods to elevate privileges.

System enumeration identified executable files with the SUID permission bit set, indicating potential privilege escalation opportunities. After exploiting the identified weakness, the attacker successfully obtained root access.

The SOC investigation reconstructed the attack by correlating Linux enumeration activity with Wazuh endpoint telemetry.

# Findings

The investigation confirmed successful privilege escalation on the compromised Linux system.

Evidence demonstrated:

- Enumeration of privileged executables.
- Identification of SUID binaries.
- Execution of sudo-related commands.
- Successful acquisition of root privileges.
- Wazuh detection and event correlation.

The attacker successfully transitioned from a standard user account to full administrative control of the target system.

# Lessons Learned

Privilege escalation frequently follows successful initial access and significantly increases the attacker's ability to compromise critical assets.

Monitoring sudo activity, tracking SUID binary abuse, and correlating endpoint telemetry with authentication events enables defenders to rapidly detect unauthorized privilege escalation attempts. Continuous monitoring of privileged operations is essential for reducing attacker dwell time and limiting the impact of a successful compromise.
